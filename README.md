# Write Every Effing Day 

Write each & every day, and make your words grow like weeds. Writing is an 
essential tool of thought, and a daily writing practice is the only way to get
substantial works written. **weed.py** is a tiny, single-file Python script for 
developing a daily free-writing practice and gamifying daily writing. 
* Unlike morning pages, it allows typing instead of writing longhand. 
* Unlike 750words.com, it stores journals locally. 

Write about anything.
* What do you need to do this day/week? Write a to-do list. 
* What are you angry about?
* What can't you let go of?
* Imagine a new life for yourself. 
* Write about the song that's stuck in your head.

Launch the app and type some words at the prompt. Save your words and update 
the word count with `Return`. You can't go back and edit: this is a feature, 
not a bug. Type `quit` or `exit` to exit early. 

Configuration happens within the file itself. 

DAILY_TARGET_WORD_COUNT: 600 or 750 words is recommended. In literary and 
academic publishing, a manuscript page is 250 words of 12-point Courier or 
Times New Roman on letter paper with 1-inch margins. A goal of 600 words/day 
is:
* 15 minutes at 40 words per minute. 
* 2.4 manuscript pages
* 2 pages of a typical trade paperback novel. 
* 1.5% of a short novel: novel in 67 days.
* ~3 pages of a screenplay (3 minutes of screen time).

While 750 words/day is: 
* 18.75 minutes at 40 words per minute. 
* 3 manuscript pages
* 3 pages of a mass-market paperback novel. 
* 1.875% of a short novel: novel in 54 days.
